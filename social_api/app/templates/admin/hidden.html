Hidden content

Content that is down but not removed — visible to its author only. An automated hide is provisional: nobody has judged it yet, and the triggering report is already closed, so this lane is the only place it surfaces. Open the title to read the full content before deciding. Restore makes it public again; remove hides it from everyone including the author, who is emailed with an appeal link.
